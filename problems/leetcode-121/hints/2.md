# Hint 2: a direction

Walking through the days in order, you meet each day once. When you arrive at a day,
the question "what is the best I could have bought on before today?" has an answer
that depends only on the earlier days, and it changes by at most one day's worth each
step.

Think about what single fact about the past you could keep, updating it as you go, so
that you never have to look back. Decide when to update it relative to the moment you
work out today's profit, and what the best should start as so that a falling list
gives the right answer.
