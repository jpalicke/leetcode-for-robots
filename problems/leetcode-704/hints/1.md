# Hint 1: what one comparison can tell you

Look at a single entry somewhere in the list and compare it with `target`.

- If it is smaller than `target`, what can you say about every entry before it?
- If it is larger, what can you say about every entry after it?
- If a comparison can rule out many entries at once, how few comparisons could you
  need for a list of 10,000 entries?

Try it on `[2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14]` with target `12`, choosing the entry to look at
yourself.
