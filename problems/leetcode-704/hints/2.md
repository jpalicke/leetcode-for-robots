# Hint 2: a direction

Keep track of the part of the list where the target could still be. At the start that
is everything. Each time you compare, you can throw away the entry you looked at and
everything on the wrong side of it, so the part that remains gets smaller.

Decide three things before you write code: how you pick the entry to look at, how you
move the edges of the remaining part, and exactly when the remaining part counts as
empty. Test your plan on a list with one entry, with the target present and absent.
