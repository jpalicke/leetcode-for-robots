# Hint 2: a direction

Reading left to right, you need to remember the openers that have not been closed
yet. Only one of them can ever matter to the next closer: the one opened most
recently. Think about a way to keep the open brackets so that the newest one is
always the first you reach, and the older ones wait underneath until it is dealt
with.

Also decide what has to be true when you reach the end of the string. Try the string
`"("` and see whether your idea gets it right.
